Detailed Project Description*
===============================================================
WelderGor (ticker: GorWeld) is a community memecoin on the Solana blockchain associated with the Dumpster Forge and GORWELD brand. The project centers on welder culture and on-chain building, using the theme “from scrap to chain.” The token is an SPL asset with 6 decimals. The official mint address is A8cDgfn1tAQbsZfD8oZU5u2xZZqKtJTmq7m9E3PLNMqr.

The token was launched on 20 July 2025 via the pump.fun launchpad on Solana. Public market data and charts are available on DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, and Jupiter. The primary trading pair is denominated against SOL on pump.fun (pair address 4vTHxks5A7PSUDZG1RrcDmYKkEC6Cy7sDMCHjUbNghqe).

On-chain configuration after launch: mint authority is renounced (disabled) and freeze authority is renounced (disabled). 50,000,000 tokens are locked under a public Streamflow vesting contract with unlock scheduled for January 2027:
https://app.streamflow.finance/contract/solana/mainnet/GbQMTJ3Wkx8ihPo1BfUXkvggqyKZMYh6HzJhhWNLW76t

Explorers and safety tools for the same mint include Solscan and RugCheck. The project maintains https://gorweld.com/ and documentation at https://gorweld.com/docs/. Official X: https://x.com/GorWeld. Discord: https://discord.gg/ufqVEeFSHm. Founder/builder: https://x.com/PrzemSas.

The canonical mint is published on the website and social channels so users can verify they trade the correct asset. The token is also discoverable with chart data in the Nightly multichain wallet after wallet-side verification.

Markets are currently on Solana decentralized venues (pump.fun bonding curve). There is no centralized exchange listing on CMC-tracked venues at the time of application. Circulating and total supply should be taken from Solana explorers / token indexers on the review date.

In summary, WelderGor is a Solana SPL memecoin with renounced mint and freeze authorities, a public long-term lock on part of supply, official web and docs, and public DEX market pages. This request asks CoinMarketCap to associate the correct contract, metadata, and market references for users.
